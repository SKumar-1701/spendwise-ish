Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 29, 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you use Spendwise-ish, we collect the information you provide directly, such as your name, email address, and password when you create an account, along with the expense records you log — amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions. We may also collect basic technical information, such as browser type and access times, to help keep the Service running reliably.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use the information you provide to operate the Service: authenticating your account, storing and displaying your expenses, and generating the spending summaries and category breakdowns you see in the app. We do not use your expense data for advertising, and we do not sell your personal information to third parties.

3. Data Storage

Your account and expense data are stored in our application database and retained for as long as your account remains active. You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time, after which your data will be permanently removed from our systems within a reasonable period.

4. Third Party Services

Spendwise-ish may rely on third-party infrastructure providers (such as hosting and font services) to operate the Service. These providers process data only as needed to deliver their service to us and are not permitted to use your data for their own purposes. We do not share your expense data with third parties for marketing purposes.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out to us through the contact details provided on our website.
